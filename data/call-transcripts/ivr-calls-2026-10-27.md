# IVR calls, 27 Oct 2026

Two calls to The Castle's line for job seekers (The Castle = the Swedish public employment service), made by Rickard by hand (speakerphone, text from AI voice mode, not Whisper).
Only the robot's side is kept here. No personnummer, no staff names, no phone numbers, nothing from the human part of the call.

Legend: **voice** = say words, **keys** = press buttons.

## What we learned

1. The menu mixes **voice** and **keys**. Every saved step needs a field for which one was used.
2. The robot names its own words: *aktivitetsrapport*, *ersättning*, *hjälp med att söka arbete*, *automatisk växel*. These are the first branches for the menu map.
3. **Long sentences fail.** Short words work better.
4. The robot can **mishear the topic** ("påanmäla" became "skriva in dig") and offer the wrong thing (SMS link).
5. **"Automatisk växel" is a trap** if you don't know a staff name. First stuck spot.
6. **Failed ID still puts you in the queue.** BankID may not be needed to reach a human.
7. A **survey question** comes right before the queue (press 1 or wait).
8. **No queue number heard** in either call. Wait was short. Queue testing needs a busier time of day.

## Product idea from the call

Nobody (robot or human) knows *where the caller is in the process* when they call. An agent that remembers the caller's state could explain it for them. Possible hackathon pitch angle.
